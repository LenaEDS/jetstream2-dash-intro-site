Getting Your Own Access
=======================

Jetstream2 is **free for researchers**. The National Science Foundation pays
for it: there are no invoices and no credit card. Usage draws down the
credits of your allocation, and shelved or idle machines consume none.

Three steps to your own cloud
-----------------------------

1. **Create a free ACCESS account.** ACCESS is NSF's coordination service
   for research resources. You sign in with your university's single
   sign-on (CILogon) at
   `identity.access-ci.org/new-user <https://identity.access-ci.org/new-user>`_.
   It takes a couple of minutes.
2. **Get an allocation.** Access to Jetstream2 comes through an ACCESS
   allocation: your PI applies, or adds you to an existing one. The
   entry-level *Explore ACCESS* tier is explicitly meant for small projects,
   thesis work, and classroom use — a dashboarding group is a natural fit.
3. **Pick your region (optional).** New allocations start in the Indiana
   University region. If you want your machines at **TACC** instead, contact
   Jetstream2 support — `help@jetstream-cloud.org
   <mailto:help@jetstream-cloud.org>`_, the
   `contact form <https://jetstream-cloud.org/contact/index.html>`_, or an
   ACCESS support ticket with *Jetstream2* selected — and ask for the TACC
   region to be enabled on your allocation.

That is the whole process. You then log in with your university credentials
— through the Exosphere web interface or the command line — and launch your
first VM.
