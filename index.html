Jetstream2 for Interactive Dashboards

Jetstream2 is a free, National Science Foundation–funded cloud for researchers. This short guide explains what it is, why it is a natural home for interactive dashboards built with tools like Plotly Dash, and how to get your own access. Each section stands on its own; the whole guide takes about five minutes to read.
