Getting Your Own Access

Jetstream2 is free for researchers. The National Science Foundation pays for it: there are no invoices and no credit card. Usage draws down the credits of your allocation, and shelved or idle machines consume none.

Three steps to your own cloud

  1. Create a free ACCESS account. ACCESS is NSF’s coordination service for research resources. You sign in with your university’s single sign-on (CILogon) at identity.access-ci.org/new-user. It takes a couple of minutes.

  2. Get an allocation. Access to Jetstream2 comes through an ACCESS allocation: your PI applies, or adds you to an existing one. The entry-level Explore ACCESS tier is explicitly meant for small projects, thesis work, and classroom use — a dashboarding group is a natural fit.

  3. Pick your region (optional). New allocations start in the Indiana University region. If you want your machines at TACC instead, contact Jetstream2 support — help@jetstream-cloud.org, the contact form, or an ACCESS support ticket with Jetstream2 selected — and ask for the TACC region to be enabled on your allocation.

That is the whole process. You then log in with your university credentials — through the Exosphere web interface or the command line — and launch your first VM.
